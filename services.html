Evaluation Factor Equipment Loan (Financing) Equipment Lease (Operating / FMV)
Asset Ownership Immediate business ownership (lien filed by lender until paid) Lessor holds title during lease term
Balance Sheet Impact Capital asset + corresponding loan liability Off-balance sheet treatment for operating leases
Tax Deductions Section 179 + Bonus Depreciation (100% write-off Yr 1) Monthly lease payments deducted as standard operating expense
Down Payment Typically 0% to 15% required Zero or low down payment (first/last month only)
End-of-Term Options Lien released; you own machine free and clear Option to purchase (FMV or $1), return asset, or renew lease
Best Suited For Long-life assets (excavators, machine tools, farm tractors) High-tech assets prone to obsolescence (IT, trucks, medical)
