Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about commercial equipment loans and broker terms.

What credit score do I need for equipment financing?
We work with institutional lenders across three credit tiers: Tier 1 (700+ FICO, prime rates from 5.75%), Tier 2 (640-699 FICO, standard rates), and Tier 3 (580-639 FICO, asset-backed programs). Because equipment financing is secured by the machine itself, approval rates are significantly higher than unsecured bank loans.
How fast can my loan be approved and funded?
Pre-qualification occurs in under 4 hours. Once your dealer quote and bank statements are confirmed, final lender credit approval is issued within 24 to 48 hours. Electronic signing and direct wire transfer to the dealer can close in as little as 1 business day.
Can I finance used equipment or private party sales?
Yes! We finance both brand new machinery from franchised dealerships and used equipment from private sellers or auctions. For used machines, lenders require serial numbers, hours/mileage verification, and photos for appraisal.
What is Section 179 and how does it save me taxes?
Section 179 allows businesses to deduct 100% of the qualifying equipment purchase price from gross taxable income in the tax year the equipment is placed in service, even if you finance the purchase with $0 down payment! Consult your CPA for specific deduction thresholds.
Does checking my terms hurt my credit score?
No! Initial intake, pre-qualification, and indicative lender term sheets are performed using a soft credit inquiry. A hard pull is only initiated after you review your offers and formally authorize closing documentation.
